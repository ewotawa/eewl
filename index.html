About

I've been knitting for about 15 years and designing my own patterns for the last two or three. Recently I decided to explore some of my favorite motifs and to sell scarves and other accessories online featuring those desings.

One of my favorite themes to explore is Ancient Egyptian art and history. As a tribute to the descendants of this culture in modern Egypt, I will donate a percentage of the proceeds from any item I sell to [CHARITY].

To see what's in stock, check out my Etsy store at the link below. See a design you like that's not in stock or want your own combination of colors? Send me a message via the contact form to request a custom piece. Thanks for stopping by!
