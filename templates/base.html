About

I've been knitting for about 15 years and designing my own patterns for the last two or three. Recently I decided to explore some of my favorite motifs and to sell scarves and other accessories online featuring those designs.

One of my favorite subjects to explore is Ancient Egyptian art and history. As a tribute to the descendants of this culture in modern Egypt, I will donate a percentage of the proceeds from any item I sell to the The Theban Mapping Project A.U.C. Library based in Luxor, Egypt. Affiliated with the Theban Mapping Project, the library gives locals access not only to books but also to literacy and educational programs.

To see ready-made items in stock, check out my store at the link below. See a design you like that's not in stock or want your own combination of colors? Send me a message via the contact form to request a custom piece. Please note that custom orders will require two to three months of lead time, depending on the size and complexity of the piece. Thanks for stopping by!
