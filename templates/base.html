About

I've been knitting for about 15 years and designing my own patterns for the last two or three. Recently I decided to explore some of my favorite motifs and to sell scarves and other accessories online featuring those desings.

One of my favorite themes to explore is Ancient Egyptian art and history. As a tribute to the descendants of this culture in modern Egypt, I will donate a percentage of the proceeds from any item I sell to the OI Cultural Heritage Initiative, affiliated with the Oriental Institute at the University of Chicago.

To see ready-made items in stock, check out my store at the link below. See a design you like that's not in stock or want your own combination of colors? Send me a message via the contact form to request a custom piece. Please note that custom orders will require two to three months of lead time, depending on the size and complexity of the piece. Thanks for stopping by!
